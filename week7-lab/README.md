# Week 7 lab: your law and its answer sheet

One file: `capstone_step1.ipynb`. Open it in Google Colab, which runs in your web browser; nothing to install.

1. Download `capstone_step1.ipynb` (this folder, or the Week 7 lab card on Tutors).
2. Go to colab.research.google.com and sign in with a Google account.
3. Click **Upload**, then **Browse**, and choose the file (or **File → Upload notebook**).
4. Click **File → Save a copy in Drive**, and work in the copy.
5. Run the cells from the top: click in a cell and press **Shift + Enter**.

Every step, with what to expect, is in the Week 7 core booklet, Chapters 4 to 6.
Part A (Ollama and tokens) is in Chapter 7. Hand in the Week 7 form on Tutors.
